TodayCare account and backup deletion

Planned for the post-review release with account and optional backup

TodayCare works without an account. Deletion is needed only when you chose to use email sign-in and optional cloud backup.

1. Delete from the app

If you can still sign in, use the fastest path:

Settings > Account and backup > Delete account

After typing the displayed confirmation phrase, your account and server backup are permanently deleted.

2. When you cannot access the app

Send a deletion request to the address below. To verify ownership, send it from the email used for TodayCare or include that email address in the message. Do not send schedule details, notification content, or verification codes.

Send an account deletion request

Suggested subject: TodayCare account deletion request

3. Information deleted

  • Your email sign-in account
  • Approved schedules you chose to back up on the server
  • Automation preferences you chose to back up on the server

4. Information that remains on this device

Account deletion removes server data only. Schedule candidates, collected records, and local schedules on this device remain. To remove device data too, use Settings > Privacy center > Delete collected data in the app.

5. Processing

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are completed within seven days after account ownership is verified. Information subject to a legal retention obligation may be kept only for that required purpose and period.
