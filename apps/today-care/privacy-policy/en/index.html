TodayCare: Schedule Alerts Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

TodayCare finds schedule candidates only from Android notifications after you consent in the app and turn on a source yourself. Complete notification text is never sent to backup, ads, billing, or sharing features.

1. Information processed on your device

After in-app consent and Android notification access are both enabled, TodayCare processes the following on your device to create schedule candidates:

  • The package name and display name of the app that sent a notification
  • For schedule candidates only, a masked title and preview, a body hash, and arrival time
  • A content-free exclusion status and deduplication data for sensitive alerts or an exclusion rule you set
  • Suggested title, date, time, category, location, confidence, and review status

Every source starts OFF on a new install. Only notifications arriving after you turn a source ON are processed.

2. Sensitive alerts and access we do not use

Notifications about verification codes, OTPs, security codes, passwords, financial accounts, card approvals, or identity documents are excluded before analysis. Their title and body are not stored.

TodayCare does not directly read the SMS database, call history, contacts, KakaoTalk internal databases, entire Gmail or Naver Mail inboxes, or existing device alarms.

3. Account and optional backup

Only if you choose Google or email-and-password sign-in in Settings, we use Supabase to provide an account. If you choose Google, the basic profile and email address provided by Google may be processed for authentication. Only approved schedule titles, categories, dates/times, locations, and approval or change times that you explicitly back up are stored in a user-isolated service for restore on another device.

Notification text, masked previews, body hashes, exclusion records, source apps, analysis reasons, and your exclusion keywords are not sent through account or backup services.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

Free users may see a Google Mobile Ads banner in Today or Calendar after adding at least one schedule. We do not place ads in candidate review, permission, privacy, or source-selection screens.

For ad delivery, frequency control, analytics, and fraud prevention, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers with Google. Ad requests start only after the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow, and you can revisit ad privacy options in the TodayCare support screen when required.

We do not pass notification content, schedule candidates, approved-schedule backups, or email addresses to ad requests.

5. Purchases and ad removal

When you buy or restore ad removal in Google Play, we use a Google Play purchase proof, product ID, and an unreadable account reference to verify the purchase on a server. Raw purchase tokens are not stored in the app or backup database.

Ad-free access is granted only after server verification. Account deletion removes the account, login information, backup, and account entitlement summary. If an active one-time purchase exists, only a SHA-256 purchase-token hash detached from your user ID, product ID, active or refund state, and processing times are retained to prevent duplicate grants and support restoration to a new account. Choosing Restore purchases from a new account asks Google Play to verify and reconnect the same purchase.

A confirmed refund or purchase revocation removes ad-free access and updates the retained status.

6. Sharing and ratings

Choosing Share or Rate in Settings opens the Android share sheet or Google Play. Shared text never includes personal schedules or notification content. A platform rating request may be attempted after you have added schedules; it uses only a local count and last-request time, not notification content.

7. Retention and deletion

General notifications and notifications that do not become final candidates are not retained. Notification-derived local data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. You can delete collection history and candidates in Settings > Privacy Center.

You can delete cloud backup in Settings > Account and backup. Account deletion removes the account and server backup; local collection data stays on the device until you remove it separately in Privacy Center.
