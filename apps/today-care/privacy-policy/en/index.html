TodayCare Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

TodayCare finds schedule candidates only from notifications for apps that you explicitly consent to and turn on. It does not store full notification text or send notification-derived data to an external server.

1. Information processed on your device

Only after you give in-app consent and allow Android notification access, TodayCare processes the following information locally on your device:

  • The package name and displayed name of the app that sent a notification
  • A masked notification title, a masked excerpt, a body hash, and arrival time for ordinary notifications
  • Schedule candidate details such as title, date, time, category, location, confidence, and status
  • Text-free listener connection and processing diagnostics

All collection sources are off on a new installation. TodayCare processes notifications from an app only after you turn that app on.

2. Information TodayCare does not directly read

  • SMS databases or SMS message contents, call logs, or contacts
  • Internal KakaoTalk databases or complete Gmail and Naver Mail inboxes
  • Existing alarms or device calendar events

3. Purpose of processing

  • To find dates, times, and tasks in notifications and present them as reviewable schedule candidates
  • To exclude authentication, financial, and security alerts before schedule analysis
  • To retain candidate actions that you add, edit, or ignore inside the app

4. Sensitive alerts

Alerts containing verification codes, OTPs, security codes, passwords, financial account information, card approvals, or identity-document terms are excluded before schedule analysis. TodayCare does not retain the title or body of these alerts. It keeps only the source app, arrival time, exclusion status, and a non-reversible hash for duplicate prevention on the device.

5. Retention and deletion

TodayCare does not store full notification titles or bodies. Masked records and schedule candidates stay on your device only and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. You can delete collected history and schedule candidates at any time in Settings > Privacy Center. Because this release has no account or cloud sync, local records do not move to a new phone.

6. Sharing, advertising, and external transfers

This version does not send or sell notification-derived data or schedule candidates to external servers, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third parties. It does not include advertising.

7. Consent and controls

Notification collection works only when both in-app consent and Android notification access are enabled. If you withdraw in-app consent, TodayCare does not store or analyze new notifications even if Android access remains enabled. You can turn each collection source on or off at any time.

TodayCare does not reply to, dismiss, or snooze notifications and does not change Do Not Disturb mode or device settings.

8. Children

This release is not designed for children and is intended for adults.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page before a material change to how the app handles user data takes effect.

This policy applies to the first-release scope of TodayCare - Smart Schedule Alerts.
